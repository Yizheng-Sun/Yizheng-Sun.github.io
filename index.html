About Me

I’m Tristan (Yizheng Sun), a PhD researcher in Computer Science at the University of Manchester, where I also earned my bachelor’s degree in Artificial Intelligence in 2023. My research focuses on the efficiency, reliability, and robustness of vision–language models, with the broader goal of making multimodal AI more dependable in practical use.

During my PhD, I developed language-guided visual token pruning methods to reduce computational cost. I also investigated how acceleration techniques, including token pruning and quantisation, change models’ responses to individual questions, even when overall performance remains similar. My work further examines how plausible but irrelevant visual information influences model reasoning.

My current research interests include:

  • Efficient multimodal AI: reducing computation while preserving model capabilities.
  • Model reliability and robustness: understanding behavioural consistency and robustness to visual distractions.
  • AI agents and recursive self-improvement: exploring how agents can learn from experience, improve their workflows, and balance the cost of self-improvement against its benefits.
